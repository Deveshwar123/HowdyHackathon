# Species photos

Put one realistic photo per species here and it replaces the illustration everywhere (species
guide and leaderboard), shown on black with only the fish visible.

- **File name:** the species id + `.webp`, `.png` or `.jpg` — e.g. `largemouth-bass.jpg`.
- **Background:** black, transparent (PNG/WebP), or plain white. A white background is removed
  automatically in the browser.
- **Framing:** the whole fish, side-on, facing left; about 1200 px wide is plenty.
- Use photos you're allowed to use (your own, public domain such as U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service
  illustrations, or CC-licensed with credit).
