      Three things make the difference

      Knuckles to the camera

      Grip the fish so the back of your hand faces the lens. Your four knuckles are the ruler.

      The whole fish, side-on

      Snout to the tip of the tail, flat to the camera. Nothing cropped, nothing pointing at the lens.

      Hand next to the fish

      Keep your hand right alongside the fish, the same distance from the camera, so both share one scale.
