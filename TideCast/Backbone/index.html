      HOW IT WORKS

      Three steps to your next great catch

      1. Find water near you. Share your location and see the lakes, rivers and bays around you, and which fish to go for.
      2. Learn what’s biting. Explore the globe in the fish atlas: every species recorded in a stretch of water, live from OBIS, with fishing activity from Global Fishing Watch.
      3. Make every catch count. Upload a photo of your catch, earn points for the species, and climb the Tidecast leaderboard.
