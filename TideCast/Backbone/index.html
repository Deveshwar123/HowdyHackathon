HOW IT WORKS

From your nearest water to the leaderboard

TRY IT NOW: LOG A CATCH →

  1. Find water near youOpen Explore the waters and tap Spots near me: the lakes, rivers and bays around you, and which fish to go for.
  2. Learn what’s bitingEvery species recorded in that water (OBIS) and fishing activity from Global Fishing Watch, on a 3D globe.
  3. Hold it, knuckles outPhotograph the whole fish side-on, gripping it so your knuckles face the camera. Your hand is the ruler.
  4. UploadAI identifies the species and traces the fish and your knuckles; you can drag any point to correct it.
  5. Earn pointsLength becomes weight on the species’ own curve, then points: rarity + 10 per pound + a trophy bonus.

To get on the leaderboard

  • The whole fish and your knuckles are visible in the photo (or you mark them yourself).
  • One photo, one entry: the same photo can’t be posted twice.
  • Sizes past a species’ record length are rejected as scale errors.
  • Keep it legal: licensed, in season and within size limits where you fish.
  • The weekly board resets every Monday; all-time keeps every catch.
