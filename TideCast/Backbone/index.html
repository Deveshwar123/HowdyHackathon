From your nearest water to the leaderboard

To get on the leaderboard

Placeholder rules
  • Create a TideCast account and allow location while fishing.
  • Upload a clear photo of each catch within 24 hours, whole fish visible.
  • The catch must be legal: licensed angler, in season, at or above size limits.
  • One fish per photo; no repeats of the same fish.
  • Weekly board resets every Monday; all-time keeps every catch.
