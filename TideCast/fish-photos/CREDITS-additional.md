# TideCast Additional Fish Images — credits and licenses

Prepared October 25, 2026. Seventeen species-specific JPGs, using the exact requested filenames. No substitute species or AI-generated fish.

All fish are whole, face left, and have been isolated onto opaque RGB white (255,255,255) canvases with margins. Original source artwork, fin details, and coloration are retained. Changes: deterministic background removal, crop and white padding, and high-quality JPEG conversion (quality 100; no chroma subsampling). Source resolution varies; no artificial enlargement or anatomy reconstruction was performed. The detached NOAA credit in gag-grouper.jpg is reproduced below instead of beside the fish. These adaptations are not official agency products; no endorsement is implied.

## Reuse status

Six USFWS source records explicitly state Public Domain. Eleven illustrations from NOAA Fisheries, Delaware DNREC and Cayuga Nature Center have no image-specific reuse license stated in the retrieved provider pages; their reuse rights remain unconfirmed. Attribution does not grant reuse permission. This folder is not certified as fully cleared for publication or redistribution. No permission inquiries were sent.

For NOAA graphics, see the [NOAA Fisheries graphics policy](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/about-us/website-policies-and-disclaimers#copyright-policy), which distinguishes government artwork from contractor and third-party material.

## Verification and availability

The exact source records identify all six species. Visual checks include sauger’s spotted dorsal fin and body blotches; gag’s marbled flanks and grouper silhouette; lake sturgeon’s scutes, barbels, and heterocercal tail; paddlefish’s broad rostrum; longnose gar’s narrow elongated snout; and freshwater drum’s deep body and long notched dorsal fin. All 17 requested species are included, including the 11 requested color replacements. No species was unavailable.
