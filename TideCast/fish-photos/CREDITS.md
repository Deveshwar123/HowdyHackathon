# TideCast Missing Fish Images — credits and licenses

Prepared October 25, 2026. Exactly the 20 requested species filenames are included; no substitute species and no AI-generated fish are used. The folder contains 20 WebP files and this credits file.

## Rights and use

Eight images have explicit public-domain or CC0 source records: the six USFWS images, the historical yellow-bass illustration, and the Guadalupe-bass photograph. Twelve NOAA, Delaware, and Oklahoma illustrations do not have an image-specific reuse license stated in their retrieved species pages. Those twelve are supplied with attribution and marked **reuse permission unconfirmed**; this collection is not certified as cleared for website publication or redistribution. No permission request has been sent to any source.

NOAA’s [graphics policy](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/about-us/website-policies-and-disclaimers#copyright-policy) distinguishes government works from third-party and contractor graphics and requires case-by-case rights confirmation. Government hosting alone does not establish public-domain status. Credit is not a substitute for permission.

## Image processing and verification

Species names were checked against the exact source records; the Guadalupe-bass record was additionally checked against original research-grade observation metadata. Each output was visually reviewed for one complete fish, left-facing orientation, visible body/fins/tail, and obvious background remnants. Images use opaque RGB white (255,255,255) canvases and lossless WebP encoding. No missing anatomy was invented. Original illustration shading, scan coloration, photographic softness, and natural pose were retained. Resolution varies with the source; no enlargement was used to claim additional detail.

The set mixes identification art, historical line art, and one specimen photograph. Cutthroat and lake trout are extracted from larger paintings and retain the artist’s slight perspective. Yellow bass retains historical sepia shading. Horizontal mirroring is recorded below. Processed images are adaptations, not official agency products; no endorsement is implied.
