Atlantic bluefin tuna

Thunnus thynnus

Built for distance. Atlantic bluefin tuna cross entire ocean basins, maintaining warm muscles that power their extraordinary swimming. They return to warm spawning grounds in the Mediterranean and Gulf of Mexico.

SIZEUp to 4.6 m
WATER ZONESurface to deep water
HABITATTemperate Atlantic & Mediterranean
Biology

A network of blood vessels helps retain heat, allowing bluefin to hunt in cold water.
